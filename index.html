Questions, answered.

How big can a single file be?

Free accounts send up to 5 GB, Pro up to 200 GB and Team up to 2 TB per transfer.

Do recipients need an account?

No. They open the link, preview the files and download. That is all.

What happens when a link expires?

The files are deleted from our servers, and the link shows a notice that the transfer has ended.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Downgrade or cancel from your account page, and you keep access until the period you paid for ends.
